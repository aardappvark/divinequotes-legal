Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 August 2026

Divine Quotes Sol does not collect, store, transmit, or process any personal data or personally identifiable information (PII). We have no servers, no databases, no analytics, no tracking, and no advertising. Your privacy is absolute.

1. Publisher Information

Divine Quotes Sol is published by ADappvark, an Australian entity operating under the laws of New South Wales, Australia.

Contact: aardappvark@proton.me

2. Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be unambiguously clear. Divine Quotes Sol does not collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Device identifiers (IMEI, advertising ID, hardware serial numbers)
  • Location data (GPS coordinates, IP-based location)
  • Usage analytics or behavioural data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data
  • Cookies, local storage tokens, or tracking pixels
  • Biometric data of any kind
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, or any other device data
  • Religious preferences, faith affiliation, or spiritual practice data

3. No Accounts

Divine Quotes Sol does not require or offer user account creation. There is no sign-up, no login, and no user profiles. The app functions entirely without any form of user registration.

4. No Analytics or Tracking

We do not use any analytics services, including but not limited to Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Mixpanel, Amplitude, or any similar service. We do not track app usage, session duration, screen views, or any other behavioural metrics.

5. No Advertising

Divine Quotes Sol contains no advertisements of any kind. We do not use advertising SDKs, ad networks, or any form of commercial promotion within the app.

6. Device Location Access

Divine Quotes Sol may request access to your device's location services for the following purposes only:

  • Prayer time calculations — to determine accurate sunrise, sunset, and prayer times based on your geographic position
  • Qibla compass direction — to calculate the bearing toward Makkah from your current location

Your coordinates are used on your device, are never sent to us, are not stored persistently, and are not shared. Location is also used to determine your country for sanctions screening: the app first reads your SIM and mobile-network country codes, and only if those are unavailable does it ask Android's own geocoding service to convert your approximate position into a country code — a platform request handled by Android (on most devices by Google), not by us. Location access is optional and the app will function without it (prayer times and compass features will be unavailable).

6.1 Permissions Declared

The App declares INTERNET, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, POST_NOTIFICATIONS and SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM. The shipped package additionally contains WAKE_LOCK, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE and FOREGROUND_SERVICE (declared by AndroidX WorkManager, which runs the daily quote notification and widget refresh) and REORDER_TASKS (declared by the Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter library). The App itself starts no foreground service. If your device does not grant the exact-alarm permission, prayer reminders are scheduled inexactly and may arrive a few minutes late.

7. Device Sensor Access

Divine Quotes Sol may access your device's magnetometer (compass sensor) and accelerometer for the sole purpose of providing compass heading functionality for the Qibla direction feature. Sensor data is processed entirely on-device, is never recorded, and is never transmitted.

8. Solana Blockchain Interactions

Divine Quotes Sol may interact with the Solana blockchain through your connected wallet application (e.g., the Solana Seeker built-in wallet). All blockchain interactions are:

  • User-initiated — the app never initiates transactions without your explicit action
  • Wallet-mediated — all signing and approval occurs within your wallet application
  • Public by nature — Solana blockchain transactions are inherently public and visible on-chain

We do not store, index, or monitor your wallet address or transaction history. Any on-chain data associated with your wallet is governed by the public nature of the Solana blockchain, not by this application.

9. Sign In With Solana (SIWS)

Divine Quotes Sol uses Sign In With Solana (SIWS) for wallet authentication. This process involves signing a message with your wallet to verify ownership. No personal data is collected during this process. The signed message contains only a domain identifier and a human-readable statement of purpose.

10. Data Storage

Your preferences (selected faith traditions, notification frequency, compass target, author-date display), your saved favourites and your local usage streak are stored in local device storage (SharedPreferences). Wallet data is stored using Android EncryptedSharedPreferences where available, with a fallback to standard SharedPreferences.

If Android Backup is enabled on your device, the App's settings and progress data are included in your device's encrypted Google backup, survive uninstalling the App, and may be restored when you reinstall. Wallet data is always excluded from backup. You can exclude the App from backup in Android Settings or delete the backup from your Google account.

11. Third-Party Services

Divine Quotes Sol does not integrate with any third-party services that collect user data. The app does not contain any third-party SDKs for analytics, advertising, social media, or data collection.

12. Children's Privacy

The app has no accounts, no advertising and no analytics, and we receive no data about any user. We comply with the Australian Privacy Act 1988, the US Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) provisions relating to minors.

13. International Compliance

ADappvark operates no backend and receives no personal data from the App, so there is no personal data for us to disclose, correct, export or delete. Deleting the App (and, if Android Backup is enabled, removing its backup from your Google account) removes all data it holds. This design is intended to satisfy:

  • Australian Privacy Act 1988 and Australian Privacy Principles (APPs)
  • EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
  • California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA)
  • Brazil's Lei Geral de Proteção de Dados (LGPD)
  • UK Data Protection Act 2018
  • Canada's Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA)
  • All other applicable data protection legislation
