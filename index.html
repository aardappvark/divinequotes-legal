About the App

Divine Quotes Sol brings together timeless wisdom from Buddhism, Christianity, Hinduism, Islam, Judaism, Sikhism, Taoism, and other spiritual traditions. All quotes are drawn from ancient scripture, traditional sayings, and authors who died in 1948 or earlier, presented with respect and reverence for every faith.

The app includes prayer time calculations and a Qibla compass feature, using location and sensor data that is processed on your device and never sent to us. If you connect a wallet, your public address is sent to a public Solana RPC provider — see the Privacy Policy.

Built for the Solana Seeker device and the Solana dApp Store. All blockchain interactions are user-initiated through your connected wallet.
