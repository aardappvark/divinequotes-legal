2.2 Deceased Authors

All quoted authors whose individual words appear in the App are deceased. No quotes from living authors are included. All quoted individuals died more than 50 years ago, ensuring public domain status under the copyright laws of Australia (life + 70 years rule under the Copyright Act 1968) and most international jurisdictions.
